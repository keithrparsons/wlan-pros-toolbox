New: SSID Airtime. A network's speed is not what it costs the air around it. This calculator shows the airtime a given SSID consumes, so you can see why adding one more network makes everything slower. The model is Jonathan Finney's, ported with his numbers and checked against his own calculator.

New: Link Info. The wired side of your connection, reported the way the Wi-Fi side always has been: which links exist, which one holds the default route, and what each one is actually carrying. Where the platform allows it, you can choose which link the other tools test over instead of accepting whatever the system picked.

New: every Field Plate downloads as a PDF from the screen it belongs to. All fourteen, no hunting.

Fixed: on a slow link, Test My Connection could tell you it had not managed to read your Wi-Fi while showing you a Wi-Fi speed on the same card. It now says which side it measured and which it did not, and offers no verdict when there is nothing to compare. Every result also names the connection it was taken over, which matters now that you can choose one.

Fixed: a saved Wi-Fi reading is dated when it was taken, not when it was reopened.

The subnet calculators are findable, and searchable by name.
