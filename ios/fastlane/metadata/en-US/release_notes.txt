New: iPhone Duo. Open the Duo and the screen splits, with the tool list on the left and the open tool on the right. Tap another tool and the right side changes while the list stays put. Fold the phone shut and the tool you had open is still there.

Half open, the split follows the fold. Held like a book, the divider sits on the fold. Held like a laptop, the tool sits above the fold and the list below.

The same two-pane view appears on any screen 600 points wide or more: an iPad, a Mac window, and most phones held sideways.

Long tool names no longer break in the middle of a word in the narrow list.

Fixed: when a Shortcut finished a Test My Connection or Wi-Fi Information run, the app could jump back to the home screen instead of showing the results.
