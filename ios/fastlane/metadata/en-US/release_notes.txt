The Wireless Classroom now has 91 tools, including 30 Guided Lessons and 50 simulators.

New: Polarization. Turn one radio wave in 3D and watch its field. Vertical, horizontal, slant, circular and elliptical, with an end-on view of the shape the field traces.

New: OFDMA vs MU-MIMO. Move clients around an 802.11ax access point and see which method needs less airtime, drawn to scale, sounding included.

New: One Talker per Channel, a Guided Lesson for beginners on why Wi-Fi slows down when everyone is home.

Association, Frame by Frame adds "Why won't it associate?": pair a client with a network and see whether they connect, and if not, the reason. Play the association now includes the 6 GHz race.

The 802.1X and EAP Ladder adds Break it: pick a fault, such as an untrusted server certificate, and watch where the exchange stops.

Rate vs Range adds a rate set builder. Antenna Pattern adds a floor view for mounting height. Multipath adds antenna diversity: compare selection and MRC with two or four antennas.

Fixed: in Channel Utilization, the ACK rate now follows the basic rate set.

The Field Manual is now the one detailed reference, with a "Teaching it" note on 76 tools. The Teacher's Guide is the short front door.
