Two fixes.

On iPhone and iPad, the signal-to-noise ratio could be reported as a negative number, and a strong connection could be graded as poor because of it. Both are corrected. If the app told you your Wi-Fi was weak on a link you knew was good, this was why.

The 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz channel allocation cards have been reissued with corrected free-space path loss figures. There is also a new 6 GHz Geofenced Variable Power card, covering the fourth power class the FCC authorized in 2026.
